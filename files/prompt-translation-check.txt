The prompt behind the translation checks (as shown on the slide "Writing the prompt that works")

WEAK
"Compare Bulgarian translation and English."
The answer could belong to anything and is discarded.

STRONG
"You are checking a finished Bulgarian translation against the English original. Both files are attached. List only the places where the meaning differs: the English, the Bulgarian, and the reason, in a table. Change nothing else."

THE FOUR PARTS: role, material, task, form.
Who it is. What it has in front of it. What is wanted. In what form.

THE TEST: could a new secretary do it?
If a competent person could not act on the instruction, neither can the system.
