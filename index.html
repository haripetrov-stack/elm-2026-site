Resources from the talk

The 30-day pilot

  1. Pick ONE documented process
  2. Measure the baseline hours
  3. Pilot with 2–3 people
  4. Compare hours AND quality
  5. Decide: scale, fix, or drop

Four guardrails (one page of policy)

  1. Human review — nothing AI-written goes out unread.
  2. Data rules — no donor/personal data in public tools; use business/no-training modes.
  3. A short list of approved tools + one named owner.
  4. Payments verified by call-back on a known number — no exceptions.
