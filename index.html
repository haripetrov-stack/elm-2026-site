Four guardrails — one page of policy

1 · Human review

Nothing AI-written goes out unread by a responsible person.

2 · Data rules

No donor or personal data in public tools; use business / no-training modes.

3 · Approved tools, one owner

A short whitelist of tools — and one named person who owns the policy.

4 · Payments: call back

Every payment request verified on a known number — no exceptions, not even for “the president on video.”
