Slide 03 · Risk we carry

When the Assistant Can Act

Full controlPermission is the real setting

Given access, an assistant can open, change and delete files, send messages and reach connected systems. The account it borrows decides how far it reaches.

UnattendedNo one is watching

Left running alone, it continues past its first mistake. A wrong instruction repeated quickly is harder to undo than a wrong sentence.

The boundaryLimits are agreed first

Decide in advance which actions need confirmation, which information stays out, and who is able to stop a run that has started.

One authorised assistant reaches further than one person's desk. Its limits are agreed before the task begins.

Slide 05 · Demonstration

Whose Voice Is It?

A labelled AI demonstration is played here in the room: a familiar face and a familiar voice saying words that were never spoken. The recording is not published. Permission from BBT covers the room only.

The point it makes: a recognisable face and a recognisable voice do not by themselves establish where the words came from. The next slide shows what can be done about that.

Slide 06 · Authenticity and proof

Digital Verification for Images, Audio and Video

Digital profiles and signaturesA digital profile for each file

Record each original’s source details and file hash. BBT Archive can digitally sign the record. A checking tool then compares the hash and verifies the signature.

Checking quotationsAI finds the source

AI finds exact passages and context in BBT Archive. A quotation absent after a full check is suspect. Voice similarity alone cannot authenticate a recording.

The prior questionShould such a tool exist?

Before we ask whether an “ask Prabhupāda” service answers accurately, we must ask whether it should exist. A synthesised reply in his person is not his instruction, and devotees may not hear the difference.

Platforms and lawPlatform action and legal rights

Content ID matches reused recordings. Rights holders can pursue infringements. EU rules require disclosure of covered deepfakes.

Approved tools should cite the Archive and separate quotation from summary. Whether a personified tool is permitted at all is a decision of its own.

Slide 07 · Internal case

AI Checks in Our Book Production

Our workflowHuman translation first

A finished chapter from a human translator, with normal editorial work. The defined checks run afterwards, never instead.

What the checks foundTwelve titles, about 2,000 findings

Findings are ordered by severity. The editor accepts or rejects each one, and the confirmed errors are fewer.

The largest case913,000 lines, 50,000 corrections

A Bulgarian hyphenation dictionary. By hand, two hours a day for three years. With these checks, two hours a day for three weeks.

The precedentThe tools have changed before

His Guru Mahārāja called the printing press the bṛhad-mṛdaṅga, the great drum. He dictated these books into a machine. The tools changed; the responsibility did not.

The machine finds. The editor decides.

Slide 10 · Practical

Writing the Prompt that Works

WeakShort generic prompt

“Compare Bulgarian translation and English.” The answer could belong to anything and is discarded.

StrongA defined task

“You are checking a finished Bulgarian translation against the English original. Both files are attached. List only the places where the meaning differs: the English, the Bulgarian, and the reason, in a table. Change nothing else.”

The four partsRole, material, task, form

Who it is. What it has in front of it. What is wanted. In what form.

A useful prompt states the role, source material, task and required format.

Slide 12 · A question we own

AI in Devotee Education

The assessmentWhose work are we assessing?

Bhakti-śāstrī and higher qualifications depend on students’ understanding. An essay alone may not establish who wrote it.

The styleFluency is not proof

Smooth prose, repeated phrasing and reading aloud can raise questions. AI can also imitate an uneven style and deliberate mistakes.

The toolsDetectors need human review

Pangram and GPTZero can flag text. False positives and missed AI text occur. A score or a hidden prompt is insufficient evidence.

Our coursesEvidence of learning

Oral defence, drafts and supervised work help assess understanding. Course leaders should define allowed AI use and disclosure.

Where is the line for our own courses, and who draws it?

Slide 13 · The human side

What It Costs the Person

The dayWork loses its edges

A subscription buys capacity, and capacity invites more hours. Morning programme, japa and rest are the first things those hours take.

The mindThe work moves into the head

Less mechanical work, more specifying, judging and checking. That is a heavier kind of tiredness and it is easy to miss in ourselves.

The heartAttachment without a person

Companion applications answer warmly at any hour, and the young are most exposed. Providers added protections after serious cases; the pull remains.

We care for devotees. Their attention and their daily sādhana are part of that care.
