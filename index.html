Risk we carry

2When the assistant can act, not only write

Full controlPermission is the real setting

Given access, an assistant can open, change and delete files, send messages and reach connected systems. The account it borrows decides how far it reaches.

UnattendedNo one is watching

Left running alone, it continues past its first mistake. A wrong instruction repeated quickly is harder to undo than a wrong sentence.

The boundaryLimits are agreed first

Decide in advance which actions need confirmation, which information stays out, and who is able to stop a run that has started.

Why it is written downA slide outlives a sentence

This risk was put on a slide on purpose. What is only spoken is forgotten; what is written can be opened again a month later.

One authorised assistant reaches further than one person's desk. Its limits are agreed before the task begins.

5Questions answered with source references

  1. A prepared demo collection. 50 files with about 23 million words, prepared for this demonstration. The manifest describes this collection's scope.
  2. One question, asked precisely. A vague question returns a thousand statements. A defined question returns a passage we can open and read.
  3. References we can inspect. Open the cited verse, letter or transcript. Read the surrounding passage and distinguish quotations from the model's summary.

This demo illustrates source checking. BBT Archive remains the reference.

  • The corpus manifestMarkdown, 27 KB · what is inside, what is deliberately outside, how the reference markers were built

To build your own, in your language:

  1. Convert his books, letters and transcripts to text. Keep only his own words; leave out the magazines, offerings and biographies.
  2. Insert a reference marker at every verse, letter and lecture heading.
  3. Pack the text into at most 50 files of under 500,000 words each.
  4. Upload to NotebookLM and let it index; on this size, that takes time.
  5. Ask plainly. Read the reference before you trust the answer.

7From our own work

Voice Chat. A question spoken aloud, in ordinary language, and an answer spoken back within seconds. Useful for ordering a difficult question before taking it to people who can decide. It can sound confident when it is wrong, so important claims are checked against their sources.

Months of specialist work, finished in days. On the website below, the assistant did the structure, the code, the page templates and the batch processing of the audio files. A person chose the recordings, the design and the moment to publish.

This does not mean every site is free, or that every temple can build one in the same time. Scope, rights to the material, accessibility, maintenance and checks all affect the effort and the cost.

The tool did the mechanical part. A person chose, checked and decided.

Practical

8Writing the prompt that works

Weak: “Compare Bulgarian translation and English.” The answer could belong to anything and is discarded.

Strong: “You are checking a finished Bulgarian translation against the English original. Both files are attached. List only the places where the meaning differs: the English, the Bulgarian, and the reason, in a table. Change nothing else.”

The four parts: role, material, task, form. Who it is. What it has in front of it. What is wanted. In what form.

A useful prompt states the role, source material, task and required format.

Bhakti-śāstrī and higher qualifications depend on students’ understanding. An essay alone may not establish who wrote it.

The styleFluency is not proof

Smooth prose, repeated phrasing and reading aloud can raise questions. AI can also imitate an uneven style and deliberate mistakes.

Authenticity and proof

Digital verification for images, audio and video

The Archive already holds the material. The useful additional work is digital processing of what exists, so that each registered original can be checked easily.

Digital profiles and signaturesA digital profile for each file

Record each original’s source details and file hash. The Archive can digitally sign the record. A checking tool then compares the hash and verifies the signature.

Checking quotationsAI finds the source

AI finds exact passages and context in the Archive. A quotation absent after a full check is suspect. Voice similarity alone cannot authenticate a recording.

The prior questionShould such a tool exist?

Before we ask whether an “ask Prabhupāda” service answers accurately, we must ask whether it should exist. A synthesised reply in his person is not his instruction, and devotees may not hear the difference.

Platforms and lawPlatform action and legal rights

Content ID matches reused recordings. Rights holders can pursue infringements. EU rules require disclosure of covered deepfakes.

Approved tools should cite the Archive and separate quotation from summary. Whether a personified tool is permitted at all is a decision of its own.

A first step: a pilot with a selected group of originals, with digital profiles, signed records and an easy check. Test exact copies, altered files and approved converted versions. Agree who certifies the records and who maintains the register.

Status: this is a proposal for additional processing and services on the existing archive. It does not claim that BBT Archive has built such a register or adopted this policy.
